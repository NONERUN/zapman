The glyph is rasterized into app.ico (white lock on a blue rounded square) for the exe file, the GUI and the tray.
dev/iconvariants makes the color variants from app.ico:
app-idle.ico: white lock on a gray rounded square;
app-warn.ico: dark lock on an amber rounded square;
app-error.ico: white lock on a red rounded square.
The source path is in app.svg.
